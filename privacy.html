Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 13, 2026

Summary

  • External links are handled separately

Definitions

"Site" refers to axmcr, a static website created by Alex Mercer. "You" refers to any visitor of the Site.

Information Collection

The Site is hosted as a static project and does not collect personal data intentionally. No forms, trackers, or analytics scripts are embedded.

Cookies

The Site does not set cookies or use local storage beyond what your browser may apply by default. (no cookies... sad)

Hosting and Infrastructure

Some pretty static hosting providers may log IP addresses, access timestamps, and basic request metadata as part of standard server operations. These logs are managed by the hosting provider and are not controlled by the site in which I am hosting here. Later, after proper upload this won't be the case.

GitHub Repository Disclosure

If the Site is hosted or mirrored through GitHub, GitHub may collect and process access data according to its own policies. GitHub’s handling of data is independent from axmcr.

External Links

External links may direct you to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. Review those policies before interacting with those services.

Data Security

No personal data is stored by the Site. Security responsibilities related to hosting infrastructure fall to the hosting provider.

User Rights

Because the Site does not collect personal data, there is no stored personal information to access, correct, or delete.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Site or hosting environment. The effective date above will be updated when changes occur.
